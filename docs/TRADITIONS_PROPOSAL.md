# Traditions (approved)

**Status: approved by Dave.** (This file keeps its old name, `TRADITIONS_PROPOSAL.md`.) The data lives in `ingest/data/`: `traditions.tsv`, `author_traditions.tsv`, `section_authors.tsv`, `contested_topics.tsv` and `perspectives.tsv`.

## The five filter traditions

1. Baptist
2. Methodist / Wesleyan
3. Presbyterian / Reformed
4. Lutheran
5. Pentecostal / Charismatic

There is no nondenominational filter. Anglican is not a filter tradition; Ryle and Fausset stay in the library as general sources.

## Contested points (11)

Baptism; the Lord's Supper; election and predestination; perseverance and falling away; Christian perfection and sanctification; end times; Sabbath and the Lord's Day; spiritual gifts today (healing, prophecy and tongues; "Tongues" merged into this); women in church leadership; creation and the age of the earth; divorce and remarriage.

Each is linked to the Nave's topics it touches: as the whole topic (e.g. "Baptism") or as part of a topic (e.g. "Marriage" for divorce). The full list is in `ingest/data/contested_topics.tsv`.

## Perspective summaries

`ingest/data/perspectives.tsv` holds a fair summary, in my own words, of each tradition's view, written so its own members would recognise it. So far it covers the two contested points used in the first lessons (baptism and spiritual gifts). They are drafts for your review.
