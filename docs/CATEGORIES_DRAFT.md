## Notes and questions for you

- **People and places make up a large share of Nave's topics,** probably more than half of the 5,322 (Nave's lists nearly every named person and place). Categories 19 and 20 will therefore be much bigger than the others. For browsing, it might help to split them further. For example: "Old Testament people / New Testament people", or "Women of the Bible" as its own category. Your call.
- **Do you want a category for Bible books?** For example, "The Gospels", "Paul's Letters", "The Psalms". Nave's has some topics for books of the Bible, but teachers usually reach books through the scripture search instead.
- **Holidays and seasons?** "Christmas", "Easter" and "Thanksgiving" aren't Nave's topics, but lay teachers plan around them. A category like "Seasons of the Church Year" could gather the relevant topics, e.g. Incarnation and Resurrection.
- **Wording:** I used "Christian Character" rather than "Virtues", and "Everyday Life in Bible Times" rather than "Customs and Antiquities". Change any wording you like.
